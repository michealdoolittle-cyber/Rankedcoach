Key Takeaways

  • Do not chase the second angle alone.
  • Call the next clear before moving.
  • Use cover as the reset point.
  • Trade timing beats hero damage.

Before: winning first contact but dying to the next angle. After: winning contact, resetting, and forcing a tradeable second fight.

  1. Lotus B Main reset behind rubble.
  2. Ascent Mid top reset after dart clears close.
  3. Bind Hookah jump peek before committing.
  1. Deathmatch: one kill, hard reset behind cover.
  2. Custom: teammate counts second swing timing.
  3. VOD review: mark every untradeable re-peek.
