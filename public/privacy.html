Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

RankedCoach provides match review, improvement tracking, and coaching-oriented analytics for competitive players. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices available to you.

RankedCoach is intended for personal performance review, self-reflection, and user-authorized coaching workflows. Riot-ID-based match sync is opt-in and limited to the features you choose to use.

Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide, such as email address, login credentials, and profile information needed to secure and operate your account.
  • Profile details you enter in the app, such as Riot ID, preferred region, profile names, theme settings, and accessibility preferences.
  • Match and rank data associated with a Riot ID you provide, retrieved on your behalf as described in "Riot-Sourced Match Data" below.
  • Gameplay-related records you import, create, or save, including manual reflections, goals, mood/comms notes, and coaching notes.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service, such as browser local storage, session data, and basic security or delivery logs created by our infrastructure.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To save your progress and sync data across sessions or devices.
  • To generate dashboards, trends, summaries, and coaching insights based on the data you choose to store or sync.
  • To maintain, improve, and troubleshoot the service.

Riot-Sourced Match Data

When you provide a Riot ID and enable match sync, RankedCoach retrieves your publicly associated Valorant match history, competitive rank, and related statistics to power its coaching features. This lookup is performed server-side through our own infrastructure — the Riot ID you provide is never sent directly from your browser to any third party.

This is a Riot-ID-based lookup, not an official Riot account authorization (Riot Sign-On / RSO). Riot's own RSO integration is not yet available for this product; if and when it becomes available, we will request your explicit permission before accessing personal Riot account data through that separate mechanism, and will only use it for features you authorize.

RankedCoach is an independent project. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by Riot Games, and Riot Games has not reviewed and does not endorse RankedCoach's use of Valorant-related data. See the notice at the bottom of this page for the full disclaimer.

Third-Party Data Processing — HenrikDev

Until official Riot API access is available, RankedCoach retrieves Riot-sourced match and rank data through HenrikDev's unofficial, community-maintained Valorant API, a third-party service independent of both RankedCoach and Riot Games. Requests to HenrikDev are made exclusively from RankedCoach's own server infrastructure using credentials that never reach your browser. HenrikDev processes the Riot ID and region you provide in order to return match history and rank data back to RankedCoach; it operates under its own terms and privacy practices, which we do not control. We do not send HenrikDev any account credentials, payment information, or data beyond what's needed to perform the lookup (Riot ID, tag, and region).

Storage and Service Providers

We use third-party infrastructure to run RankedCoach, including:

  • Supabase — account authentication, encrypted database storage for profiles, saved matches, and reflections, and our Ask Coach and private knowledge-analysis functions.
  • Cloudflare — application hosting, edge request routing, and secret management for server-side API credentials.
  • OpenAI — server-side analysis used to produce structured coaching notes from public educational-video captions and to support AI coaching features. Knowledge-analysis requests disable provider-side response storage.
  • HenrikDev — third-party Riot-sourced match/rank data retrieval, described above.

These providers may support additional functions such as content delivery, communications, and — if enabled in the future — payments. We do not share more data with any provider than is needed for them to perform their function for us.

Educational Video Research

RankedCoach may process publicly available metadata and caption text from educational videos already referenced in the Library Playlist. This processing happens server-side to create private, timestamped context notes for the owner review queue. Full transcripts and private context notes are not exposed through the public knowledge endpoint. Only owner-reviewed, original RankedCoach guidance may be published to players; published records omit transcript text and private analysis metadata.

Cookies and Local Storage

RankedCoach may use browser local storage or similar technologies to preserve settings, local app state, and session-related preferences. These technologies help the site function and improve usability.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data with the service providers listed above, only as needed for them to operate the app on our behalf, and we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the integrity and security of the service.

Security

Account data is stored using our providers' encryption at rest and in transit. Server-side credentials for third-party services (including HenrikDev) are stored as environment secrets and are never included in code delivered to your browser. RankedCoach supports optional multi-factor authentication for account sign-in, available in Account & Support settings. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention and Deletion

We keep account and app data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to operate the service. Synced Riot match data is retained to power trend and history features; it reflects only what our upstream data source (HenrikDev, or Riot directly once RSO is available) currently retains, not a guaranteed complete history. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting support@rankedcoach.gg.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to sign in, whether to enter a Riot ID, and whether to use guest mode or synced features. Entering a Riot ID and enabling sync is optional and can be disabled at any time from Account & Support settings. If Riot Sign-On is enabled later, you will also be able to choose whether to authorize that separate account linking.

Children's Privacy

RankedCoach is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@rankedcoach.gg. Product feedback and bug reports can be sent to feedback@rankedcoach.gg. Business inquiries can be sent to business@rankedcoach.gg.
