How transcript passages are chosen RankedCoach keeps repeatable player actions and preparation choices—setups, decisions, timing, spacing, utility, positioning, practice, settings, adaptations, and punishments. Team standings, season form, roster stories, qualification, and match or series results are excluded unless the passage explains the transferable action itself.
